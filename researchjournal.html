introduction to this page

10/24/2024 tags: #misc



welcome to my research journal!!! for me, i tend to have a lot of thoughts about a lot of different things: geography, secret counties, interesting fonts, photos, the way in which people do and think and talk… but a lot of those end up being tabs open on my computer more than anything. what a shame… but that’s what this is for. i have so many things to share, and i don’t plan on yapping, but more so maybe sharing my thoughts but also facts….


for example, id love to talk about my dear favorite island country tuvalu… but also some ex-soviet countries like bulgaria, and some interesting literature set there, like cleanliness by garth greenwell. artists i follow on twitter, blogs i found that haven’t been updated since 2006, and many more things. i hope i can update soon and i hope you will enjoy


lastly, it will be formatted this way: an entry will be made on a topic, and if i have more to add, it will be added in the same page under a different date. if the topic is the same but i feel it needs its own entry, it will get it own…that’s really it. yay!!!!
