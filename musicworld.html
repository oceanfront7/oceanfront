welcome to my music page

this is my music page! i would definitely consider myself a musicophile (if that's even a word). i listen to an average of 21,000 tracks per year. i've finally risen to 51 average scrobbles a day! im addicted to checking my stats on last.fm.

i listen to more tracks than i do artists, so i have a lot of varying artists where i listen to maybe 1-2 of their songs. really, i listen to music moreso for the genre than any specific artist. for example, im not really a k-pop 'fan' anymore, but wow i definitely still listen to k-pop a lot. but it's all like 1 song from each group lmfao

regardless, music is such a big part of my life, idk what i would do without pretending im in a music video every single day. on this page you can find my current favorites (songs, artists, albums, etc.) of the month, my top albums, artists, songs, and a little journal section where i write about the concerts i've been to. enjoy!
